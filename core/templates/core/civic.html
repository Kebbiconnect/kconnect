How a concern becomes a campaign

  1. A community member reports a concern.
  2. KPN reviewers check and document it. Published concerns appear on Community Voice, labelled as community-raised with their verification level.
  3. Where the evidence supports it, the concern informs further reporting — or becomes an advocacy campaign with a defined objective and a clear proposed action.
