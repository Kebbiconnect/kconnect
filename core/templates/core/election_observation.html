What observers do

Our trained observers monitor polling units across Kebbi State to ensure free, fair, and credible elections. Observers watch the process; they do not take part in it, and they do not speak for any candidate or party.

Becoming an observer

KPN observers are members of the Civic Observer Network. Join the network to be considered when KPN formally observes an election.
